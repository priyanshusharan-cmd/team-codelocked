## Events (Utsav 2026, all past)

| Event | Date | Venue | Team size | Prize pool |
|-------|------|-------|-----------|------------|
| **The Last Block** | 17 Apr 2026 | PJ Block, 4th Floor | 3–4 | ₹4,000 |
| **Mind Snap** | 18 Apr 2026 | PJ Block, 4th Floor | 3–4 | ₹4,500 |
| **The Challenge Room** | 19 Apr 2026 | PJ Block, 3rd Floor, CR-301 | 3–4 | ₹4,500 |

**The Last Block:** a Jenga mystery in three rounds.
1. **Clue Extraction:** teams pull blocks that hide clues
2. **Connect the Dots:** teams put the evidence together and discuss possible solutions
3. **Final Reveal:** teams present their conclusions

**Mind Snap:** memory and reflexes.
1. **Memory Blackout:** recall images and patterns after they disappear
2. **Capture the Clue:** a photo treasure hunt across campus
3. **1-Minute Challenge:** short games, quizzes and quick tasks

**The Challenge Room:** four teamwork rounds.
1. **Blindfold Memory Mayhem:** teammates split observation, recall and guidance
2. **Sensory Showdown:** identify objects and solve puzzles without sight
3. **QR Chaos:** QR codes around the venue link into one bigger puzzle
4. **The Pivot Challenge:** a digital escape room

Each event has a poster. No gallery photos have been added.

## Their design (for reference, not to copy)

- Dark theme. Background `#0a1017`, text `#f1f5f4`, accent mint `#95dacb`, muted text `#acbac3`
- Fonts: Geist (body), Instrument Serif italic (display headings), monospace for labels
- The nav is a rounded "pill" bar. Event cards show the poster and a Date / Venue / Team / Prize grid. Each page has a breadcrumb label, like "TeamCodeLocked / Our people".
