# Voice Card and Fact Sheet

This is the copy layer for the TeamCodeLocked website, written in Phase 1 using the taste skill's `copy-voice.md`. Every page's copy is written from this file. The voice card is for writers only and never appears on the site.

---

## Voice card

```
Voice: Facts only (register 1), with Hurried & conversational (register 12) in the microcopy
Pairs: specific, not stiff · friendly, not chummy · proud, not boastful
Person: "we" for the club and its work; "you" for the student reading; events and BitByBit as the subject in their own sections
Sentences: mostly 6–14 words. One longer one per section, where it explains how something works.
Humour: none in the hero, the event facts, the contact details or the join steps. Allowed in empty states, the 404 puzzle and one aside per page, always with a fact right after it.
House words: the club's own nouns, used the same way every time: Utsav, BitByBit, the rounds by their real names (Clue Extraction, Memory Blackout, QR Chaos…), OC, the Core Committee, PJ Block, Club Orientation
Never: virtue words about ourselves (passionate, dedicated, innovative, dynamic), a strip of invented figures, headings split into two fragments, "X, not Y" more than once per page, puns on "locked" (one use per site), any word from the banned list in WEBSITE_PROMPT.md §4.5
```

**Why this voice:** a club with real events, real people and its own platform has strong facts, and facts-only copy works best for clubs when the facts are good. The conversational edge in the small text makes it sound like students wrote it. The voice to avoid is the model default (warm, wry, a twist at the end of every paragraph), because readers now recognise it as AI.

**Voice holds, tone moves:**
- The hero and the event stories carry the most personality.
- Join steps, contacts and event details stay plain. People reading them want answers.
- Errors and confirmations are short and kind: "Copied." "That code didn't open the lock. Try the event dates."
